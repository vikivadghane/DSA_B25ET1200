FAQ 1.What are the different types of expressions? Ans: There are three types of expressions: Infix, Prefix, and Postfix. They differ in the position of the operator relative to the operands.
2.Give examples of all types of expressions. Ans: Infix: A+B – The operator is between the operands. Prefix: +AB – The operator is before the operands. Postfix: AB+ – The operator is after the operands.

3.Which data structure is used for evaluation of expressions? Ans: A Stack is used for expression evaluation. It follows the LIFO (Last In, First Out) principle and helps process operands and operators in the correct order.

4.Convert the infix expression A+B+C+D to prefix expression. Ans: The given infix expression is A+B+C+D. In prefix notation, operators are written before their operands. Therefore, the prefix expression is +++ABCD.

5.Convert the infix expression A+B+C+D to postfix expression. Ans: The given infix expression is A+B+C+D. In postfix notation, operators are written after their operands. Therefore, the postfix expression is AB+C+D+.
